# Internal access policy

PrimeContractorOS has two internal accounts that do not require a paid customer subscription:

- `dominiquereed35@gmail.com` — sole PrimeContractorOS platform owner. This identity receives platform-owner access and may bypass workspace subscription gating.
- `reedssolutionsllc@gmail.com` — Reeds Solutions LLC business account. This identity may bypass subscription gating only for a workspace it owns; it does not receive platform-owner authority.

All other customer accounts remain subject to normal subscription, trial, grace-period, and platform-override rules.
